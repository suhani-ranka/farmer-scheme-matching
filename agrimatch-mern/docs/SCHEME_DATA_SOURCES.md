# Scheme Data Sources and Verification Notes

**Catalogue review date:** 22 November 2026. The date indicates when the source pages were checked for this educational starter. It does not mean every live application window, district target, notification or individual eligibility condition is guaranteed current. Re-check before public use or submission.

## Source review process for future additions
1. Prefer the scheme's official portal, department website, government notification or official state scheme dashboard.
2. Record the scheme's government level and exact applicable state(s).
3. Capture the source URL, source title and date checked.
4. Separate stable scheme purpose from changing details such as benefit amounts, deadlines, crops, district targets and required documents.
5. If a detail is not confirmed, say “Check current official notification” instead of guessing.
6. Do not scrape or republish unofficial summaries as verified facts.
7. Revisit records periodically and mark them `needs-recheck` when a source changes or cannot be reached.

## Why the seed data is deliberately limited
India has many Central and State agriculture schemes. This project includes a curated sample to demonstrate the multi-state data model. It does not claim to be a complete national scheme database.
